Agave Lounge creates big, joyful shared experiences that lift people up and build culture, community and civic spirit.

About 130 campers raise a 70-foot geodesic dome and run a comfy lounge with mezcal and tequila cocktails, daytime activities and house music.

Inside you'll find cold craft cocktails, a bouncy castle, giant bean bags and a soft, furry lounge to sink into.

Everyone is welcome: kick back, swing in a hammock, bounce, dance, and grab a drink at the bar if you're 21+.

Daytime brings offbeat workshops like roller yoga, meow-meditation, jazzercise, a twerkshop and a ratchet-strap class.

House music runs 3–9pm, and that's when the dome fills up with dancers.

Our custom-built sound system carries a lineup of favorite house, disco and funk DJs.

We span ages, backgrounds, orientations and skills, and keep working to widen that mix.

Campers join from San Francisco, Santa Barbara, San Diego, Tahoe, Los Angeles, New York and beyond.
