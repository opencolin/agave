Culture

Our camp culture is rooted in the 10 Principles of Burning Man, written by co-founder Larry Harvey in 2004 for the Regional Network as a reflection of the community's ethos. Here's how we live them.

Leaving No Trace

We clean up after ourselves and others, raking for every last bit of MOOP.

Shoes Off Inside

Keep the dome clean and comfy for everyone.

Humility

We show up to serve, not to be served.

Gifting

We gift the spirits of the agave plant: cold mezcal and tequila cocktails plus other drinks, over 30,000 of them in 2022.

Diversity

All ages, ethnicities, genders, orientations and countries are welcome. Financial assistance covers dues for those who need it, and we interview every new camper so expectations line up.

Decommodification

No brand promotion, only the agave. Bottle labels get covered with our sticker, and the dome is built by our own crew.

Self-reliance

Every camper is encouraged to grow and lean on their own resources.

Self-expression

Each person's unique gifts are theirs to share, with respect for others.

Communal Effort

We value cooperation and build shared spaces together.

Civic Responsibility

We look out for public welfare and run our events within the law.

Participation

Everyone works and everyone plays. Change happens by doing.

Immediacy

We value being fully present with each other and the playa.
