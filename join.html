Camp with us

Agave Lounge runs on everyone pitching in. We work together, play together, and each camper owns their own participation.

Pick when you work and what you do. Every camper commits to four 4-hour shifts across these areas:

  • Build: before or in the first days of the event.
  • Leave No Trace: keeping camp clean during and after.
  • Activities: running what we offer the city (bartending is a favorite).
  • Strike: teardown, packing the trailer and hauling trash.
